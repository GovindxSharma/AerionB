About Aerion Medtech
- Nature of Business: Healthcare innovation company connecting medical institutions with advanced, certified technologies from trusted global and local sources.
- Mission: To make healthcare more efficient, innovative, and accessible through precise procurement today and responsible manufacturing in the future.
- Vision: To lead transformation in healthcare by introducing solutions that are smarter, more robust, and more accessible.
- Founder: Aarya Lekhadia – Biomedical engineer educated at University of Pennsylvania and Penn State. Expertise in biomedical devices, healthcare systems, and product development.
- Specialization: Custom procurement of medical devices for hospitals, clinics, and providers; focus on relevance, usability, safety, and trust.
